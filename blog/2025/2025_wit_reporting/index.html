We completed 568 acres of mechanical treatment in 2025. These acres will may not count toward WIT because we only consider treatments in ponderosa pine that seek to create openings, clumps and groups as wildlife treatments. Treatments that strictly seek to meet a basal area, while effective for fuels mitigation, do very little to improve or maintain habitat for wildlife and therefore are not counted.

We have one more treatment, Turkey Springs, that has not been added to FACTS yet and I was not able to pull into this report.
