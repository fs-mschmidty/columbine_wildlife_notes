# Term Overview

The primary goals for the term were to survey timber sales for raptor nests, complete annual aberts surveys, and map sensitive species habitat and distribution around project areas.

## Raptor Surveys

We surveyed two ponderosa pine forest timber sale areas to ensure no nests would be disrupted.

### Junction Creek

Over several weeks we surveyed 22 timber units, performing broadcast acoustic surveys at 179 survey points. Although no nests were found, several raptors were observed including a peregrine falcon (known nest on falls creek cliff face), a juvenile golden eagle, several coopers hawks, and many red tailed hawks.

### Abert's Squirrel Survey

Over several visits inturrupted by hail and lightning storms, we completed an annual Abert's survey at Dry Fork.
